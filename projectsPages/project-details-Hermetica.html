Key Contributions

  • Balanced the game’s combat difficulty using spreadsheets and rational design methodologies to fine-tune over 75 enemies featuring rock-paper-scissors–style mechanic, achieving a more balanced player experience that increased player engagement by 23%.
  • Developed a data-gathering method based on csv files for game balancing and tuning.
  • Designed and implemented core systems, including inventory management, item functionality, and in-game codices.
  • Owned the design, implementation, and ongoing iteration of the game feel, including feedback systems, camera effects, controls, and moment-to-moment interactions.

    Among the game-feel effects I implemented for the player’s altered states are:

    • Low Health: Added red pulsing vignette, intensified heartbeat SFX, subtle lens distortion with heartbeat-synced screen pulses, weakened camera stability, and desaturated color grading to convey critical condition.
    • Poisoned: Implemented purple vein-like screen pulses that filled and faded rhythmically, matching the stylized visual treatment applied to the poisoned character, supported by subtle distortion cues and accompanied by a sharp camera shake representing the insertion of the poison.
    • Acid Exposure: Implemented corrosive droplet impacts with dripping particle overlays, sizzling SFX, and hard camera jitter.
    • Burning (Fire Damage): Created a paper-burning screen effect inspired by Hermetica’s art style, with edges darkening and curling inward, enhanced by ember flickers, crackling fire SFX to enhance the sense of scorching damage, and a wide, panic-like camera shake modulation to convey the intense pain and spread of the flames.
    • Injured (Broken Bone): Added sharp vignette flashes, stagger animations, restricted movement responsiveness, and crunch-like impact SFX.
    • Dizzy: Applied a dizzying spiral overlay, screen-edge distortion, and a dark green vignette to visually convey disorientation, accompanied by subtle ringing/echo SFX.
    • Cursed: Implemented a vampire-themed curse effect, with sharp fangs biting into the screen, darkened vignette, shadowy artifacts, and subtle whispering ambient SFX to reinforce the ominous sensation.
    • Bleeding: Added red directional streak overlays, heartbeat-synced screen pulses, weakened camera stability, and progressive vignette darkening.
    • Sluggish (Slow Debuff): Introduced reduced camera responsiveness, softened motion blur trails, and delayed input feedback to emphasize heaviness.
  • Worked within and expanded 5+ existing systems (resources system, shop system, HUD, tutorial…) integrating feedback from player testing to provide a satisfactory and excellent gaming experience.
  • Designed, implemented and iterated on the game tutorial by making it more concise and funnier, ensuring that players understood the core mechanics of the game, improving player retention.
  • Prefab setup, hierarchy implementation and Scriptable Objects configuration.
  • Coded some tools to streamline the development team’s workflow.
